Did You Know? - Fun Facts

Space Fact

A day on Venus is longer than its year. Venus takes 243 Earth days to rotate once on its axis, but only 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun.

Animal Fact

Octopuses have three hearts. Two pump blood through the gills, while the third pumps blood to the rest of the body.

History Fact

The Great Pyramid of Giza was the tallest building in the world for over 3,800 years.

Science Fact

There are more stars in the universe than grains of sand on all of Earth's beaches combined.
